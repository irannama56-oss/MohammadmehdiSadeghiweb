## Trailing-slash gotcha (important)
Adding `trailingSlash: false` to vercel.json broke the prebuilt sub-apps that use
relative asset URLs (konkortimer-dist `./_next/`, currency-converter `./assets/`,
css-playground `script.js`) — they rendered blank. Removed the setting and restored the
trailing slash on exactly those three URLs in projects.json / mini-projects.json.
Rule: a project folder served under /Projects/** needs the slash in `url` if its
index.html references assets relatively; Vite builds with an absolute `base` (novabank,
cryptoproject) do not.
